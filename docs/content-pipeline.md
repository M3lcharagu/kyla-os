## Production workflow

1. **Select and stage:** Work through the queue in order. Keep source footage, audio, thumbnail frame, and caption idea together in one project folder.
2. **Edit on iPhone 11:** Use **CapCut** for the cut, timing, audio, captions, and export. Use **Canva** for a consistent cover/thumbnail and reusable KYLA templates.
3. **Set the canvas:** Vertical **1080 × 1920**, **15–30 seconds**, with the subject inside the safe center area for TikTok and IG overlays.
4. **Hook immediately:** Put the strongest visual and/or lyric on screen in the first **2 seconds**. Start with a short text hook such as “This scene still hits 😮‍💨” or “Wait for the drop 🔥”.
5. **Cut to the beat:** Sync cuts, zooms, and transitions to the track; target a change every **1–2 seconds**. Remove pauses and dead frames.
6. **Caption for retention:** Use large, high-contrast captions with short lines and relevant emojis. Keep text clear of the bottom UI area and proofread names/titles.
7. **Brand and export:** Apply the **KYLA** watermark/branding consistently (small, readable, low obstruction). Review the full clip, then export a clean 1080 × 1920 vertical master from CapCut.
8. **Package and publish:** Add the Canva cover, a one-line caption, and a hashtag mix. Post **2 anime + 1 celebrity daily**, in the **7:00–10:00 pm Kenya time** window. Reply to early comments and log performance.

## Hashtag mix

Use a balanced set rather than a copy-paste block:

- **Niche:** `#animeedit #animeclips #animetiktok` or the relevant show/character tag.
- **Celebrity/topic:** the person's name plus a relevant culture, music, or creator tag.
- **Format/brand:** `#edit #fyp #reels #tiktokkenya #KYLA`.

Use the most relevant tags for the specific edit, rotate combinations, and avoid unrelated or misleading tags.
